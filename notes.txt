# 8/4/26: Another day, Another learning experience

Alrighty I got my own IDT working now. So I can handle Interruptions fine. Now my uptime command works properly too!
I also added Shutdown/reboot commands to my OS so now I can properly shutdown stuff too. But I havent binded it to 
my shutdown key. After doing a tiny bit of research I figured out theres something called a FADT (Fixed ACPI Descriptor Table)
and inside that FADT there are two specific I/O ports. The ports are called PM1a_CNT_BLK (This is where the shutdown command will be)
and PM1a_EVT_BLK (This is where the power button flag lives). Now I wont be making the shutdown functions binded to my Power button
yet. But that is just to know what and where it is. Now I need to make a system clock and hardware stats reader thingy. I really
do not want to deal with more ASM...
